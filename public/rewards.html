Who's eligible

You need a linked wallet (verified signature), no review flag, and at least — CHIP mined in compute epochs this season. The top — make the list. Share is each wallet's cut of a pro-rata payout before any per-wallet cap.

How it's paid

The operator exports this list and runs the memcoinz airdrop from the fee-funded rewards wallet, pro-rata by CHIPs mined. Motherlode hits get their own payout from the motherlode wallet. Every batch signature goes into a ledger that can't double-pay.

Motherlode hits

Each hit pays the miners on the winning rack, by the power they had there. Each one gets its own fee-funded payout list.

Provably fair

Check any epoch yourself

Each epoch's seed is fixed in advance by a hash chain. The server publishes that chain's last link, the anchor, before play, and every revealed seed hashes back to it. The seed is mixed with the drand quicknet round published after the epoch closes, which no one can know in advance. Verify fetches that round straight from drand and recomputes the winning rack in your browser.
