    How an epoch pays

    01

    Deploy

    Spread free power ⚡ over any racks before the epoch closes. Power is free and regenerates while you play. It's never bought or sold, and it has no cash value.

    02

    Draw

    One rack wins. Its miners get the power from the other 24 racks, minus a 10% burn, split by stake. They also get +1 CHIP: half the epochs split it, the other half send it to one top miner.

    03

    Earn

    When the week ends, the top — linked miners (at least — CHIP) share the $CSTR fee pot by CHIPs mined. Motherlode winners get a separate fee-funded jackpot. All of it is on the public list.

    No house edge on the draw

    Provably fair, every epoch

    Committed

    Every seed comes from a hash chain fixed when the game launched. The commit for each epoch is shown before it opens, so the server can't choose a seed once deploys are in.

    Unpredictable

    The seed is mixed with the drand beacon round published after the epoch closes. drand is signed by the League of Entropy, and nobody, including this server, knows it in advance.

    Checkable

    After settlement the seed, drand round and signature are published. The audit page recomputes the winning rack in your browser from drand's own API.

    FAQ

    Is this gambling? Do I risk money?

    No. You play with free power that can't be bought, sold or withdrawn. Rewards come from $CSTR trading fees and go to the season's top miners. A version where you stake real SOL is a separate decision and isn't live.

    Can the server rig the draw?

    No. The server is locked into its seeds in advance by a hash chain, and the drand round it mixes in doesn't exist until after the epoch closes. If drand can't be reached within 5 minutes, the epoch is void and every deploy is refunded.

    Can I bot it?

    All deploys are checked on the server against your real power. Power only regenerates while you're online and is capped. One handle per wallet, one wallet per handle, and sign-ups are rate-limited. Accounts that look like farms are flagged and left off the payout list until they're reviewed.

    What happened to the clicker?

    CSTR is now compute epochs. Your handle, tag and linked wallet carried over. Your old clicker total is kept as a legacy badge, and the new season score is CHIPs mined.

    Does linking a wallet cost anything?

    No. You sign a plain text message, which sends no transaction and costs no fee. This site never asks for a seed phrase or private key. If anything does, close it.

    CSTR

    Power ⚡ and CHIP are free in-game units: they aren't purchasable, transferable or redeemable. $CSTR rewards come from trading fees, are paid at the operator's discretion, and can change or stop at any time. Nothing here is financial advice or an investment offer.
