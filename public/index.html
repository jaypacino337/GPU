FAQ

Can I bot or edit my save?

No. Your browser only sends inputs. The server works out idle income from its own clock, refuses purchases you can't afford, and drops clicks above the cap. Sustained clicking at the cap, or flooding past it, flags the account. Flagged accounts can still play but are left off the payout list until they're reviewed.

What happened to my old save?

Saves from before seasons were reported by the browser, so nobody could verify them. Your lifetime total is kept as a legacy badge, and your run restarts under verified rules.

Do I need to hold $CSTR?

Not for the leaderboard pot. The operator can also run a holders-and-players round, which pays only wallets that are both on the top list and hold $CSTR, weighted by balance. Each round's rules are posted before it's paid.

Does linking a wallet cost anything?

No. You sign a plain text message, which sends no transaction and costs no fee. This site never asks for a seed phrase or private key. If anything does, close it.

When does a season end?

Seasons are weekly, in UTC. The countdown at the top shows the time left. Your game keeps going between seasons, but the season score starts again from zero.
